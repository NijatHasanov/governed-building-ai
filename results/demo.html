How it works


The AI proposes an action every 15 minutes: a cooling setpoint and a fresh-air rate, chosen to minimise predicted cost over the next two hours. A governance layer checks each proposal against the rules, corrects anything that breaks a hard rule, relaxes soft rules only when a hard rule needs it, and writes a plain-language explanation.


In recommendation mode, staff review the AI's changes. When they override one, the AI's preferences are updated on site, so it needs fewer corrections the next day.


Prototype v0.1, open source (MIT). Single-zone model of a 600 m2 school block with 120 occupants over three synthetic hot-season days; all parameters are illustrative and listed in the code, not measured from a real building. Version 0.1.0.
